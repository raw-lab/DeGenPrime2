Third-party licences for src/mafft
==================================

src/mafft is a vendored copy of rust-MAFFT 0.2.0
(https://github.com/luksgrin/rust-MAFFT), a Rust port of MAFFT
(https://mafft.cbrc.jp/alignment/software/). rust-MAFFT is licensed
"MIT AND BSD-3-Clause": its own Rust code is under the MIT licence and the

licence. Both notices are reproduced below, as they require.

These licences apply to src/mafft only. The rest of DeGenPrime is under the
licence in the LICENSE file at the top of the repository.
